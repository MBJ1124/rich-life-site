Support

Rich Life requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Need a hand with Rich Life? Most answers are below. If yours isn't, email richlifehabits.app@gmail.com. I read every message.

Questions

How do I restore my Rich Life Plus purchase?
  1. Open Rich Life and go to Settings.
  2. In the Rich Life Plus card, tap Restore purchases.

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy Plus. Restoring is always free, on a new iPhone or after reinstalling. You'll also find Restore purchases on the Plus screen, right under the buy button.

How do I get my diamonds and Shop items back on a new phone?

If you're signed into iCloud, Rich Life backs up your diamond balance and the Shop items you own, wear and have earned to your own iCloud. To get them back:

  1. Sign in to the new iPhone with the same Apple ID, with iCloud turned on.
  2. Install Rich Life and open it. Your diamonds and items come back on their own, usually within a few seconds.

Your habits, streaks and coins stay on the phone they were made on and aren't part of this backup, so on a new phone your character starts a fresh climb. A full iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer) brings everything along, the same as your other apps.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can cancel anytime:

  • In Rich Life: Settings → Manage subscription.
  • Or on your iPhone: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Rich Life.

Plus stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. If you cancel during the 7-day free trial on the yearly plan, you won't be charged.

How do I turn off anonymous usage stats?

Open Rich Life, go to Settings, and turn off Share anonymous usage stats. From then on nothing is sent. These stats are only counts, like "a habit was checked off", never your habit names or anything you type. More in the privacy policy.

Why did my net worth go down?

Every day has a small cost of living, shown at the bottom of Today on the Home screen. It settles at midnight. On days you keep your habits you earn more than it costs; on quiet days the balance slowly slides. It never goes below zero, and one good day starts the climb again.

How do I contact you?

Email richlifehabits.app@gmail.com, or use Settings → Contact support in the app. If something isn't working, it helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version (Settings → General → About), and what you were doing when it happened.

Please never send passwords or payment details. Rich Life never needs them.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

The short version: Rich Life keeps your habits and progress on your device. There's no account. The app sends only anonymous usage stats, which you can turn off in Settings. Your diamonds and Shop items are backed up to your own iCloud. Apple handles every payment. No ads, no tracking.

What we collect

Anonymous usage statistics, and nothing that identifies you. To learn which parts of the app help people and where they get stuck, Rich Life uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. It receives:

  • Usage data (product interaction): counts of things done in the app, such as a welcome screen reached, a habit checked off, the paywall opened (and from where), a purchase made, a tier reached, a Shop item tried on. Each comes with app facts only, such as the screen, plan, item or tier. Never your habit names, notes, or anything you type.
  • A device identifier: a random ID created for this install, which TelemetryDeck scrambles (hashes) on your device and again on its servers, so it can't be traced back to you or your device.

This data is not linked to your identity, not used for tracking or advertising, and never sold or shared. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats; then nothing is sent.

  • No account. There is no sign-up, login, or profile.
  • No servers of our own. Everything works offline; usage statistics wait and send later.
  • No ads and no tracking. No advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, no data brokers.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Rich Life (your habits, completions, streaks, coin balance, and character choice) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data permanently.

One exception, kept in your own iCloud: if you're signed into iCloud, your diamond balance and the Shop items you own, wear and have earned are backed up to your private iCloud storage, so they come back if you reinstall the app or get a new phone. The developer can't see this data; it never leaves your iCloud account. Your habits, completions and coins are not backed up.

Purchases (Rich Life Plus and diamond packs)

Rich Life offers an optional upgrade, Rich Life Plus, as a subscription or a one-time purchase, and optional diamond packs for the Shop. All purchases are made through Apple's App Store using your Apple ID. Apple processes the payment and handles receipts, refunds, and cancellations; the developer never sees your payment details. The app checks your purchase status on your device using Apple's StoreKit. No purchase information is sent to any server operated by the developer.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, Rich Life schedules local notifications on your device using Apple's standard system. No notification content or token ever touches a server, because there is no server.

Children

Rich Life is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children; the anonymous usage statistics above are the same for everyone and can be turned off in Settings.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics, or tracking, and it doesn't load anything from other companies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, it will be updated here and the app's App Store listing will reflect it before the change ships.

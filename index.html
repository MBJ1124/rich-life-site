Rich Life

Keep your habits. Watch a life get richer. Every habit you check off earns coins for your character, and their whole world levels up with you.

Coming soon to the App Store iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Your habits, made visible

Pick Jay, Nia, Sam or Rose. Add your real habits. Then watch what showing up does to their life.

Habits pay coins

Drink water, work out, read for ten minutes. Check one off and the coins fly to your balance. Harder habits pay more.

Streaks compound

Keep a habit going and its payout grows, up to 50% more. Small daily wins add up to a rich life.

Life has a cost

Each day takes a small cost of living from the balance, a little more as life gets bigger. Skip a few days and the good life slowly slips.

A pendant that tells the story

Every character wears a pendant that upgrades with their wealth: a plain chain, then silver, then gold, then diamond.

The Shop

Earn diamonds for good days, perfect days, streaks and new levels. Spend them on hats, glasses, a cat or a dog, celebrations, faces and voices. Try anything on first.

Kind by design

Falling behind is a slow slide, never a cliff and never a guilt trip. Gentle reminders at the time you choose, and no ads.

Rich Life Plus

Rich Life is free: one character (your pick of four), up to 5 habits, streaks, the wealth chart and basic stats, reminders, and home and lock-screen widgets. Plus adds:

  • Unlimited habits
  • Quests: one-time goals worth 100 coins
  • Goal-based habit library
  • Switch between all four characters
  • Streak freezes
  • Advanced stats
  • Themes & app icons
  • 100 diamonds every month for the Shop

$4.99 a month, $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $49.99 once. Payments are handled by Apple.

Your habits stay yours

No account and no sign-up. Your habits stay on your iPhone. Diamonds and Shop items are backed up to your own iCloud. Only anonymous usage stats are sent, and you can turn them off. No ads, no tracking.
